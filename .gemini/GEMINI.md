## Path & Documentation Overrides
1. **Brainstorming**: Always update `docs/specs/` with results, decisions, and `WORKPLAN.md` progress. This overrides the global `docs/brainstorm/` requirement.
2. **Architecture**: Keep `docs/arch.md` updated as the primary technical reference.

## Commit Approval
When requesting commit or push approval, provide a "glimpse of changes":
- A concise summary of the work accomplished.
- A list of all modified and newly created files (mimicking a `git status` overview).
- This summary acts as confirmation that relevant documentation in `docs/` and `docs/specs/` has been updated.
